       - Integrate `executeStepWithHealing` from `electron/core/healing/selfHealingRunner.ts`.
       - When a step is healed during `run_flow`:
         * Call `saveHealArtifacts` to capture screenshot and DOM snapshot in `test-results/<flow-slug>/`.
         * If `flow.filePath` or `flow.path` is available and `autoHeal` is enabled (default: true), call `patchYamlFile(filePath, stepIndex, result.healingDetails.healedSelector, { confidence: result.healingDetails.confidence })`.

         * Call `saveFailureArtifacts` to record screenshot and DOM snapshot.
         * Broadcast step update with `status: 'failed'`, `errorMessage`, and artifact paths.
